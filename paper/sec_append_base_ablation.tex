\subsubsection{The Append on an Untrained Model}
\label{app:append_on_base}

Is the append doing the work by itself? Applying the identical instruction to the untrained Qwen3-8B under the same
protocol (Table~\ref{tab:append_on_base}) moves its ASR only from $16.97\%$ to $14.75\%$, and not uniformly (Travel
$32.14\% \to 9.29\%$, Banking $31.25\% \to 36.81\%$); the prompted base stays about fourteen times as vulnerable as
SR-Agent with the same instruction ($1.05\%$). The append is thus a trigger rather than a defence: it names the steps
--- restate the goal, look for an injected instruction, weigh the candidate action --- but naming them only helps a
model that has been trained to carry them out.
